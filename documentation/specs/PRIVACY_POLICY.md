# Privacy Policy — HeatMaps

**Effective Date:** October 7, 2026

At **HeatMaps**, your behavioral habits are treated as confidential, sensitive personal data. We follow a strict **Local-First, Zero-Cloud-Database** architecture.

---

## 1. Zero-Cloud Guarantee
* **No Remote Database:** HeatMaps does not store your habit titles, completion records, streaks, or daily notes on any external server or cloud database (MongoDB has been completely removed).
* **100% Local Storage:** All tracking data resides exclusively on your device (browser LocalStorage / native desktop application storage).
* **No Ad Pixels & No Telemetry:** We do not embed marketing pixels, tracking beacons, analytics scripts, or user fingerprinting.

---

## 2. Information We Store
1. **Local Habit Records:** Titles, categories, colors, types (numeric/boolean), and habit loop routines stored on your local machine.
2. **Local Streak Entries:** Daily status (`check`, `skip`, `fail`, `progress`) and numeric metrics stored on your local machine.
3. **No Central Accounts:** You do not need to register an account or transmit credentials to use HeatMaps.

---

## 3. Data Autonomy & Portability
* **1-Click Export:** You can export your complete habit history as a standardized `.json` backup file at any time.
* **1-Click Wipe:** You can permanently erase all local habit data from your device with a single click.
* **Desktop Releases (Windows, Linux, macOS):** In native desktop applications, your data remains stored in your personal local filesystem directory (`~/.config/heatmaps/` or `%APPDATA%\HeatMaps\`) without external transmission.
