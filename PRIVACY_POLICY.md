# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 7, 2026

At **Habo Web** ("Habo", "we", "us"), privacy is not an afterthought—it is the bedrock of our software. We believe that your personal routines, mental health habits, and daily behaviors are deeply intimate information that belongs strictly to you.

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how it is handled, and your rights under GDPR, CCPA, and global privacy standards.

---

## 1. Our Privacy Commitments
1. **Zero Data Brokering:** We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your habits, notes, or usage patterns to advertisers or data brokers.
2. **Minimal Data Collection:** We collect only the absolute minimum information required to operate a functional habit-tracking web application.
3. **No Third-Party Analytics Trackers:** We do not embed invasive marketing pixels, Facebook trackers, or cross-site tracking cookies.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide
- **Account Credentials:** When you register, we collect your email address and a cryptographically hashed password. If you use OAuth (Google / GitHub), we receive your basic verified profile name and email address.
- **Habit Tracking Data:** Habit titles, descriptions, categories, completion statuses (done, fail, skip, progress), numeric metrics (e.g., 20 pushups), and personal check-in notes.

### B. Technical & Infrastructure Data
- **Session Cookies:** We use secure HTTP-only cookies strictly for authentication and session continuity.
- **Server Logs:** Standard ephemeral server access logs (IP address, browser user-agent, timestamp) maintained temporarily (up to 7 days) solely for DDoS mitigation, rate limiting, and server diagnostics.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use your information exclusively to:
- Authenticate your access to your habits.
- Render your 52-week GitHub-style streak box heatmaps and calculate streaks.
- Enforce the "Two-Day Rule" and streak algorithms.
- Allow you to export backups or migrate your data.
- Maintain application security, prevent abuse, and deliver critical security notices.

---

## 4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Processors
We share data only with infrastructure sub-processors essential to hosting the application:

| Sub-Processor | Role | Location & Compliance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **MongoDB Atlas** | Managed Database Hosting | Encrypted at rest (AES-256) & in transit (TLS 1.3), SOC 2, ISO 27001 |
| **Vercel / Cloudflare** | Edge Hosting, CDN & DDoS Shield | SOC 2 Type II, Global Edge Network |

We do **not** grant any sub-processor the right to use your habit data for machine learning training, profiling, or advertising.

---

## 5. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Regardless of where you reside, you possess the following rights:
1. **Right to Access & Portability:** You can download a complete JSON export of all your habits, check-ins, and notes at any time via your Settings page.
2. **Right to Rectification:** You can edit or correct any habit or log entry at any time.
3. **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** You can delete any individual habit or delete your entire account permanently. When you delete your account, all associated database records are irreversibly purged within 30 days.
4. **Right to Restrict Processing:** You may opt out of any non-essential communications or revoke OAuth access.

---

## 6. Security of Your Data
We implement enterprise-grade security standards to protect your information:
- End-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption in transit.
- AES-256 encryption at rest in our databases.
- Strict Content Security Policy (CSP), anti-CSRF token mechanisms, and HSTS headers.
- Regular security audits and dependency vulnerability scanning.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy
Habo Web is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will immediately take steps to delete such data.

---

## 8. Updates to this Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When significant updates occur, we will notify registered users via an in-app banner or email notification before the changes take effect.
